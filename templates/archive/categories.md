# Categories

Each category is one line starting with a hyphen: the slug, a dash, then a description. The description is also what the classifier decides by, so say clearly what the category covers and, if needed, what it doesn't. Replace these starter categories with your own.

- ai-agents — AI agents and agent frameworks: how they are built, run and evaluated.
- ai-models — New AI models, benchmarks and research results; not products built on top of them.
- developer-tools — Programming tools, libraries and engineering practices.
- tech-business — Companies, funding, strategy and the market around technology.
- other — Posts with a clear topic that fits no other category yet.
- unavailable — Posts whose content could not be fetched, because they were deleted or the account is private.
